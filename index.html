03 — About

I like turning ideas into things people actually use.

I’m a student and engineer who builds the whole stack: how the model behaves, the app in your hand and the servers behind it. Most of my work sits where AI meets the real world. That means agents you can check instead of blindly trust, native software that feels at home on the device, and infrastructure that keeps both honest once real people start using them.

Currently

Building in stealth: an AI product that runs across phones, the cloud and, eventually, hardware.
