03 — About

I like turning ideas into things people actually use.

I started with HTML, CSS and Java, went deep on Python in my school’s engineering academy, and took a UC Berkeley boot camp on big data in AI in 2023. Since then I’ve built games, a reinforcement-learning racer, a compliance AI and a genetics-based diet planner, and now I’m working on something in stealth. I care about the whole thing working end to end, not just the demo.

  1. 01

    Prototype fast

    Get a working version in front of real people early, then iterate.

  2. 02

    Test end to end

    If the full pipeline isn’t tested, it isn’t done.

  3. 03

    Never fake the data

    No made-up numbers. If something isn’t known, say so.
